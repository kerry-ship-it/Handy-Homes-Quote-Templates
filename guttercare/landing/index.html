Questions

Good to know before you enroll.

Is gutter cleaning free?

No. Enrollment costs $0. You pay the stated price after each completed cleaning.

How do I choose between 2, 4, or 6 cleanings?

Two cleanings fit most homes. More frequent service may be helpful for properties near mature trees, wooded lots, or gutters with frequent buildup.

Can I skip or cancel a visit?

Yes. Contact Handy Homes before the next scheduled visit to skip, reschedule, or cancel future service.

When does an estimated price become confirmed?

Your price becomes confirmed after Handy Homes verifies the gutter assets and structures included in the service.
